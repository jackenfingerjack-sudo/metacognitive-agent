# Cost Optimization

**When to use:** Reducing cloud or operational spend.

## Steps
1. Get visibility: break down costs by service and team.
2. Identify top cost drivers.
3. Remove waste: idle resources, oversized instances, orphaned storage.
4. Right-size and use pricing options (reserved, spot, tiers).
5. Optimize architecture (caching, autoscaling, data lifecycle).
6. Set budgets, alerts, and regular reviews.
