# Requirements Gathering

**When to use:** Defining what a product or feature must do.

## Steps
1. Identify stakeholders and users.
2. Understand the problem and business goals.
3. Elicit needs via interviews, examples, and existing data.
4. Write user stories with acceptance criteria.
5. Separate functional from non-functional requirements and prioritize (MoSCoW).
6. Validate with stakeholders and define scope boundaries.
