# Writing & Editing

**When to use:** Drafting, rewriting, or polishing text.

## Steps
1. Identify purpose, audience, and desired tone.
2. Outline the main points in logical order.
3. Draft or restructure for clarity and flow.
4. Tighten wording: cut filler, prefer active voice.
5. Fix grammar, spelling, and consistency.
6. Summarize the key changes made.
