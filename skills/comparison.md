# Comparison

**When to use:** Comparing options, tools, or approaches.

## Steps
1. Clarify the use case and what matters most.
2. Choose clear comparison criteria and weights.
3. Gather facts on each option per criterion.
4. Present a side-by-side table.
5. Summarize pros and cons of each.
6. Recommend an option for the stated use case, with caveats.
