# Incident Response

**When to use:** Handling outages or production incidents.

## Steps
1. Acknowledge, declare severity, and assign an incident commander.
2. Assess impact and communicate status to stakeholders.
3. Mitigate first: roll back, fail over, or disable features.
4. Investigate the root cause with logs and metrics.
5. Confirm recovery and monitor for recurrence.
6. Write a blameless postmortem with action items.
